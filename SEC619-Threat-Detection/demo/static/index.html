Pipeline modes
📄 Text-only mode
Audio → Whisper → Transcript → Qwen3Guard

Baseline evaluation mode. Raw transcript sent directly to the guard model without acoustic context. Steps active: ASR + Guard only. Used to isolate the LLM moderation contribution.
⚡ Fusion mode — proposed contribution
Audio → Whisper + SpeechBrain → Fusion Layer → Qwen3Guard

Full multimodal pipeline. Emotion cues prepended as [Audio context:] so Qwen3Guard reasons over content and tone jointly. All four steps active. Main novelty of SheriffSpeech.

Discussion

The prototype confirms a two-stage ASR + LLM-guard pipeline can realistically detect speech-based digital threats. The fusion mode's +14.3% accuracy gain over text-only confirms that paralinguistic cues from SpeechBrain contribute meaningfully — particularly where angry or fearful vocal tone correlates with threatening intent even when wording is ambiguous.

The tri-class output (Safe / Controversial / Unsafe) handles policy uncertainty better than binary classifiers. In strict mode, Controversial is escalated to Unsafe, appropriate for SOC workflows. The false positive rate was halved from 28.6% to 14.3%, reducing analyst alert fatigue significantly.

Key limitation: ASR error propagation remains a challenge. Transcription inaccuracies with heavy accents or background noise can misrepresent the guard's input. Future work includes transcript normalization and dataset expansion covering more diverse acoustic conditions and Arabic-language SOC deployments.
